## Featured project

### [Customer Discovery Tool](https://github.com/N0rmal1/Customer-Discovery-Tool)

**From a business category and location to an organized research sheet.**

Find local businesses with Google Places, enrich their details, and export the results to Google Sheets—ready for review and follow-up.

- **Find and organize:** search across a city and deduplicate businesses by Place ID.
- **Keep useful context:** collect websites, phone numbers, ratings, and addresses.
- **Preserve your work:** retain manual notes and CRM fields when rerunning imports.
- **Plan API usage:** preview estimated costs and track runs in a local log.

**Built with:** Python, Google Places API, Geocoding API, and Google Sheets API.
